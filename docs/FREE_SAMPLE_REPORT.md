# Free 5-Domain TrustLint Sample Report

TrustLint CLI is **open source and free to run locally** on any number of domains — no limits, no license key, no subscription required.

The free 5-domain sample report is a **lead-capture and service preview**. It is not a CLI restriction. It is designed for web agencies, freelancers, small SaaS teams, and DevOps/security consultants who want to preview a client-ready TLS risk review before commissioning a full audit.

## Who It Is For

- **Web agencies** managing client domains
- **Freelancers** managing client websites
- **Small SaaS teams** preparing for launch
- **DevOps and security consultants** who want a sample client-ready TLS risk review

## What Is Included

- Up to 5 domains
- Summary ALLOW / REVIEW / DENY results
- Key TLS and certificate findings
- Basic recommended actions
- Short email or Markdown-style summary

## What Is Not Included

- No full security audit
- No penetration test
- No compliance certification
- No security guarantee
- No white-label formatting
- No recurring monitoring
- No scan history
- No Slack or email alerts
- No full remediation plan
- No PDF deliverable (unless manually offered later)

## Responsible Use

You must **own or be authorized to scan** the submitted domains. Do not submit domains you do not control or do not have permission to test.
